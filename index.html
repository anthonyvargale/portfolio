Full structural design and analysis of a 64-storey,380m in height, mixed-use office tower connected to the PATH, with a team of five. Acting Technical Lead for Structural Scheme Option 1, a concrete shear wall core with perimeter gravity columns, including the ETABS model, preliminary design of bracing / framing members, shear core, and gravit members.

  • Compiled dead, superimposed and reduced live loads and building seismic weight per NBCC load cases.
  • Preliminary column sizing with a conservative allowance for unsized self-weight.
  • Compared various floor systems flat plate, slab with beams and post-tensioned floor systems on thickness, self-weight, punching shear and floor-to-floor height.
  • Designed preliminary steel framing per CSA S16, considering connection types, framing orentation, and overall weight of floor system to optimze weight of building
  • Developing Foundation Layout, and integrating new superstructure elements with retained 5-level substructure, evaulating stability and backstay forces.
