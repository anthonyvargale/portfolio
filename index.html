Sample Calculation

Capstone · Typical floor framing

Gravity Framing Check, Typical Office Floor

A hand calculation for a typical floor of the capstone tower, set out the way I'd hand it to a reviewer:

  1. Design basis: NBCC loads, CSA A23.3, materials and assumptions stated up front.
  2. Loads: dead, superimposed dead and live loads, with tributary areas.
  3. Load combinations: 1.4D and 1.25D + 1.5L, governing case identified.
  4. Member design: flexure, shear and punching shear checks.
  5. Verification: hand results compared against the ETABS model.
